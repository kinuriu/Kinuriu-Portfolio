Patient No-Show Risk Predictor

An AI-powered automation that scores each patient's no-show risk and logs a plain-language reason and suggested intervention, built entirely no-code on Make.com.

The problem

No-shows are a quiet cost in most clinics: lost slots, wasted prep time, and no consistent way to flag who's actually at risk before the day of the appointment.

What it does

Pulls appointment and patient data from Google Sheets, sends it to Claude (via OpenRouter) for risk analysis, and logs the result to Notion for clinical review.

Input signals

Age, days between scheduling and appointment, SMS reminder status, prior no-show history, and health factors (diabetes, hypertension, alcoholism, handicap access needs).

Output

A Risk Score (High / Medium / Low), a one-sentence Risk Reason, and a one-sentence Suggested Action, plain language a front-desk coordinator can act on immediately.

Stack: Make.com · Claude via OpenRouter · Google Sheets · Notion

Patient Intake-to-Payment RCM Automation

A self-hosted n8n workflow automating the front end of Revenue Cycle Management: intake, eligibility verification, referral verification, and prior authorization. Tested end to end on synthetic data, not a full RCM pipeline yet, and it isn't meant to be one yet.

The problem

Intake, eligibility checks, referral verification, and prior authorization are usually four separate manual tasks with no shared record of what was checked, when, or by whom.

What it does

Receives intake data, masks PHI before logging, checks eligibility against a mock insurance API, and routes each patient: auto-approved, flagged for manual review, or logged as an error.

Scope, honestly

Covers stages 1–4 of a 15-stage revenue cycle. Scheduling, claims, adjudication, and billing are documented as planned future work, not built. Synthetic data only, never real PHI.

What's tested

Eligibility-based routing, the auto-approve / manual-review split, error handling on a failed API call, and referral/authorization routing across all four combinations.

Stack: n8n (self-hosted, Docker) · PostgreSQL · Redis · MinIO · a mock insurance API
